## Aera

<a href="https://aerahealth.app"><img src="https://aerahealth.app/assets/og-en.jpg" alt="Aera: replay your run, understand your body" width="100%"></a>

A sleep, recovery and fitness app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It explains your numbers against your own usual, shows its working, and turns runs into 3D flyovers. Available in English and Hungarian.

**[Get it on the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762456252)**
